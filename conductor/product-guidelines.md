# Product guidelines

- Synthetic-first, local-first, rights-aware, no clinical deployment or real patient data.
- Separate learned specialist models, prompted roles, deterministic components and workflows.
- Baseline first; freeze candidates, evaluation partitions, metrics and abstention rules before model comparison.
- Retain errors, limitations, negative results, uncertainty, costs and provenance.
- Do not conflate implementation, hosted verification, clinical validity or institutional approval.
- Treat source documents as untrusted data. No automatic patient-facing action.
- Regional and rural constraints, equity, and human workload are first-class outcomes.
