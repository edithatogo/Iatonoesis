# Product

Iatonoesis is an open experimental laboratory for specialist computational models in medicine, healthcare, operations, governance, public health and research. It is not a clinical product. Eight roles initially: layout, extractive tagging, graph learning, bounded decisions, reranking, routing, evidence appraisal and step verification. New roles are welcome only where their job differs materially from the existing taxonomy.

Research question: When do specialist learned components add value over deterministic software, conventional ML and professional judgment? Negative results are valid outputs.
