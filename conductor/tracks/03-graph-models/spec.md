# T03 — Graph models

Owner: [#4](https://github.com/edithatogo/Iatonoesis/issues/4). Research-only. Canonical tasks: [programme](../../../contracts/programme.v1.json).

## E07: Rural dependency resilience

Classification: adaptation; setting: rural operations.

Workflow: Managers test contingency options.

Input: synthetic sites, rosters, credentials and equipment. Output: dependencies and cascade predictions.

Comparator: capacity table and deterministic graph traversal. Failure and reviewer: double-booked specialist; site managers reconcile.

Feasibility cohort: five sites and 30 disruptions. Model metric: dependency recall and disruption accuracy. Workflow outcome: simulated cancellation/transfer counts.

## E08: Polypharmacy signals

Classification: documented underlying capability; setting: patient safety research.

Workflow: Pharmacologist investigates signal, not prescribing decisions.

Input: public rights-clear or synthetic drug/event graph. Output: ranked associations and supporting paths.

Comparator: interaction rules and disproportionality/link scores. Failure and reviewer: confounding by indication; epidemiologist checks.

Feasibility cohort: temporal held-out associations. Model metric: PR-AUC and later signal recall. Workflow outcome: investigation time per credible signal.

## E09: Complementary cohort discovery

Classification: hypothesis; setting: research.

Workflow: Investigators evaluate reuse before recruitment.

Input: public study metadata and synthetic linkage. Output: ranked cohort links and opportunities.

Comparator: keyword and deterministic traversal. Failure and reviewer: double-counted cohort; data custodian verifies.

Feasibility cohort: 50–100 studies or synthetic analogues. Model metric: recall@10 and false independence. Workflow outcome: viable questions and acquisition effort.

All claims and research progress remain unverified until the corresponding evidence is captured.
