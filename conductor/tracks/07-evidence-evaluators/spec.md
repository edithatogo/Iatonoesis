# T07 — Evidence evaluators

Owner: [#8](https://github.com/edithatogo/Iatonoesis/issues/8). Research-only. Canonical tasks: [programme](../../../contracts/programme.v1.json).

## E19: Risk-of-bias appraisal

Classification: documented underlying capability; setting: research.

Workflow: Methodologist verifies judgment and passage.

Input: rights-clear trial reports or synthetic excerpts. Output: domain appraisal, supporting span and uncertainty.

Comparator: checklist and keyword guidance. Failure and reviewer: randomisation assertion accepted without method; reviewer corrects.

Feasibility cohort: 80 examples. Model metric: weighted kappa and evidence precision. Workflow outcome: review time.

## E20: Rural evidence transferability

Classification: adaptation; setting: rural service planning.

Workflow: Committee reviews implementation prerequisites.

Input: public evidence and synthetic site profile. Output: indirectness, missing capability and unresolved risks.

Comparator: standard checklist. Failure and reviewer: overlooked rescue capability; local expert reviews.

Feasibility cohort: 40 synthetic proposals. Model metric: limitation recall and unsupported recommendation. Workflow outcome: preparation and rework.

## E21: Organisational causal claims

Classification: hypothesis; setting: health economics.

Workflow: Analyst investigates insufficient causal evidence.

Input: simulated interventions, trends, designs and confounders. Output: claim appraisal and missing assumptions.

Comparator: dashboard and design checklist. Failure and reviewer: regression to mean ignored; epidemiologist checks.

Feasibility cohort: 100 simulated evaluations. Model metric: false causal endorsement and calibration. Workflow outcome: review time and unsupported claims.

All claims and research progress remain unverified until the corresponding evidence is captured.
