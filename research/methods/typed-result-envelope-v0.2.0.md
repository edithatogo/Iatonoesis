# Typed result envelope v0.2.0

Refs T00.002/T00.003/T00.005 (#1), T14.004 (#15), T15/T16 (#16/#17), T18 (#19) and T05/T07 (#6/#8). Backward-compatible extension of the existing set-based runner: preserve its v0.1.0 contract and all earlier receipts. The new trusted local adapter runner validates the same dataset and experiment contracts, adds metric_kind/metric_options and emits a typed v0.2.0 manifest. It independently recomputes metrics from frozen references and ordered prediction arrays; checks config/dataset/reference/split/prediction digests and arm completeness; validates model provenance and timing. Adapters receive copies of inputs, not gold. Unavailable/failed arms never receive success metrics; partial failures retained separately for diagnosis. No preemptive process sandbox; trusted callables only. Dataset rights and independently verified training isolation remain separate obligations.

- Continuous: scalar prediction, or explicitly configured {value,lower,upper}; MAE/MSE/RMSE and observed interval coverage/width on scored cases.
- Ranking: complete fixed judged candidate pool with reference qrels and k; macro nDCG/precision/recall and explicit defined denominators. Unjudged items rejected; no silent assumption that unjudged means irrelevant.
- Calibration: binary independent reference and probability in [0,1]; Brier, ECE and bins; no claim of external calibration.
- Mutation: per-case reference signatures, candidate pool size, budget; selected IDs, deduplicated non-equivalent signatures, macro coverage and detected/total counts. Distinguish decision coverage from mutation coverage.

For all kinds total/scored/abstained and decision_coverage are recorded; undefined metrics are null. Continuous/probability/coverage metrics are selective on scored cases, never claim a high selective score is automatically superior at lower coverage. Comparative statistical procedures and group-aware intervals are separate, not silently pooled. No clinical data artifacts may be published. No independent clinical or canonical task acceptance claimed.
