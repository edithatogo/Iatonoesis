For every new source: verify terms at component level, primary data and annotation revision, exact bytes/hashes, source grouping and label origin; record unknowns. Seal IDs and split manifests before model inference, record no train/calibration/test overlap by group, quarantine pretraining or previously exposed cases. Independent source does not imply independent adjudication or no contamination. Keep raw restricted inputs outside Git/public artifacts. For TrialGPT, establish meaning of 881 training-flag rows and underlying rights before choosing group-disjoint development/calibration/test or external test-only; do not manufacture independence. For PubTables, select a fixed rights-cleared sample by deterministic source identifiers before inspection. Retain original published splits and duplicate checks. For step verification distinguish independent CQL scalar outputs from true clinical outcomes. Until qualification, report candidate, structural-audit, frozen-test-only and executed-exploratory separately.

Hybrid protocol: compare deterministic, conventional learned, specialist neural, guarded neural, calibrated fallback and agreement under fixed evidence/candidate/review budgets. Include an explicit independent human-review arm only with approval. Report effective coverage, exact correct, unsafe false pass/closure, missed evidence, fallback activation, incremental latency and cost; never credit an abstention as successful care. In exactly computable tasks retain exact solver/graph/rules. Introduce new independent challenge groups with uncertain evidence, counterexamples, unobserved contexts and mismatched source layouts. Predeclare primary and safety endpoints, calibration thresholds, budget, frozen evaluation and uncertainty; no test-based tuning. Negative results retained. This is not acceptance of T14 or any clinical deployment.
