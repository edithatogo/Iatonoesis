# T18.004 — unlabelled target performance estimation, synthetic pilot

Exploratory, frozen before execution. Refs #19 #16 #1 #12 #15. Reuse the source-only ridge reconstruction at zero acquisitions from T15.001 (seed 7139, 480 development and 160 calibration subjects); prior held-out test subjects are not used. Unitless synthetic measurements, no clinical inference.

Create 20 development, 20 calibration and 20 held-out environments, each with 64 distinct generated subjects. Seeds 18000+i, 18100+i, 18200+i. Development/calibration vary one of missingness, process noise and mean drift at a time using fixed grid; test has different seeds and combined shifts, some outside development support. Freeze mechanisms, feature map, group split, model and comparator settings before evaluation. Meta estimator sees only aggregate history features (mean and SD of last observed measurement, average observed fraction, average absolute slope, lag, regime) in unlabelled target environments; no individual targets or hidden simulator parameters in features. Meta development labels are actual source predictor MAE and source-calibrated 90% interval coverage. Comparators: constant source calibration performance; constant development environment mean; three-nearest-environment mean in standardized observed features; ridge fitted to the same labelled development environments, penalty 1. Clip estimates to valid support. Separately calibrate 90% absolute estimation-error intervals over the 20 calibration environments. Primary absolute error of estimated target MAE; secondary absolute coverage-estimation error and empirical interval coverage by held-out environment. Report the environment count, never treat 1,280 subjects as independent environment units. No inferential clinical claims or post-hoc tuning.

Negative control: two fresh environments with byte-identical visible histories but a +3 hidden shift to measured outcomes; identical estimates must not be presented as identification of the true error. Show actual change in performance and note non-identifiability under conditional/outcome shift. Models cannot establish transportability without target labels or assumptions. This is not independent clinical or real cohort evidence, nor a task acceptance. Reproduce twice and record raw digest; source-only predictor must remain frozen, training uses development meta environments only. Additional target settings and PhysioNet cohort/actual site qualification needed.
