## Primary measured time evidence and estimand boundaries

- Dall'Ora C, Griffiths P, Hope J, et al. International Journal of Nursing Studies. 2021;118:103921. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijnurstu.2021.103921), [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8249906/), Table 3. Time-motion observation on 16 English hospital wards; modelled arithmetic means per vital-sign set: bedside excluding non-vital-sign interruptions 225 s (95% CI 212–238); round excluding non-vital-sign interruptions 301 s (279–324); round including interruptions 386 s (361–410). Different estimands, not pooled. Preparation shared across round patients; not independently additive components. No Australian or ICU-specific marginal cost inferred.
- Wong D, et al. A ward-based time study of paper and electronic documentation for recording vital sign observations. JAMIA 2017;24:717–721. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28339626/). 577 events; geometric means 215 s (177–262) paper and 150 s (130–172) electronic; heterogeneous ward effects. Keep separate descriptive context; geometric means are not arithmetic expected total staff time.

These are complete observation workflow times, often including HR itself. The prior benchmark masks HR and reveals selected other values, not real clinical ordering. A real full bundle may directly reveal the target; the following is explicitly a counterfactual proxy and not an observation-ordering or monitoring recommendation.

## Prospective resource-use instrument (draft, no collection initiated)

For an independently authorised time-and-motion study, record separately: site/service and care setting; pseudonymous round/episode grouping in approved storage; staff category and task start/end; direct person-minutes versus elapsed time and overlapping work; routine required versus additional assessment; access to existing electronic value versus new individual measurement versus bundled set; bundle content, target already directly available; equipment sourcing/availability, preparation, travel, measurement, documentation, verification/rework, interruption reason/duration; equipment/consumable and compute/network costs; staff grade and loaded unit-cost source/year/perspective; missing/censored timing and resource failure. Report shared preparation once per round, not once per item. Explicitly account for review burden, transfer/travel and telehealth separately; do not impute rural premiums. Keep mandatory clinical observations outside an optional AI acquisition policy, never defer indicated monitoring, and do not record clinical data or staff/patient identifiers in this public repository. Before actual work: institutional determination and local workforce/consumer review; sampling strata for rurality, setting, staffing and technology; prespecified incrementality and uncertainty, validation of observation categories, clustering by ward/round/staff as appropriate.
